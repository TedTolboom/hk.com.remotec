Control your air conditioner and trigger scenes with Remotec Z-Wave devices.

The ZXT-120 AC Extender and the ZXT-600 AC Master turn Z-Wave commands into the infrared signals of your split air conditioner. Set the mode, target temperature, fan mode and swing from Homey, read the room temperature, and use the built-in IR code library or learn the codes of your original remote.

The Scene Master ZRC-90 gives your flows 8 buttons that each react to a single press, a double press, holding down and releasing.
